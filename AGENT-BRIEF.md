# Agent brief — US legislation on AI in courts, evidence & legal services (read AGENT-BRIEF-COMMON.md first, then `schema/bills.schema.json`)

**Lane rule (strict).** Include a bill or enacted law only if an **operative section** does one of: `evidence` (AI-generated/deepfake/machine-generated evidence in proceedings; authentication duties; e.g. Louisiana Act 250 (HB 178, 2025); California SB 11 (2025, vetoed)); `courts-judiciary` (AI use by courts/judges/clerks; court AI task forces created by statute; e-filing AI disclosure; e.g. H.R. 7997 / S. 4154 (119th) Research and Oversight of AI in Courts Act); `legal-services-upl` (AI legal services, chatbots giving legal advice, UPL by software, consumer legal AI; e.g. NY S7263A/A6545C; Utah SB 226 (2025) as it names legal services); `attorney-ai-use` (disclosure, verification or competence duties for lawyers/arbitrators; e.g. **California SB 574 (2025–26)** — enrolled 2026-09-04, on the Governor's desk); `police-reports-criminal` (AI-drafted police reports and disclosure to courts/defendants — California SB 524 (2025), Utah SB 180 (2025); forensic-algorithm access — Justice in Forensic Algorithms Act; facial recognition in proceedings — Maryland SB 182 (2024)); `legal-aid-a2j` (AI in legal aid/self-help funded or regulated by statute); `court-records-training` (statutes on bulk court-record access for AI training). **Exclude** general AI regulation (TRAIGA, Colorado AI Act, SB 243 chatbots, NO FAKES, TAKE IT DOWN, election deepfakes) unless a section is in-lane — record excluded near-misses in `work/excluded.jsonl` with the rule cited so the next run does not re-check them. Cover **2023–2026 sessions**, all 50 states + DC + Congress.

**Sources.** LegiScan Public API (CC BY 4.0; key in `work/LEGISCAN_KEY` if present, otherwise use `getSearch`-equivalent via the public search pages sparingly and Open States): `https://api.legiscan.com/?key=KEY&op=getSearch&state=ALL&query=%22artificial+intelligence%22+AND+(evidence+OR+court+OR+attorney+OR+%22practice+of+law%22+OR+%22police+report%22)&year=2` then `getBill`/`getBillText`. Open States v3 (`https://v3.openstates.org/bills?q=...` key in `work/OPENSTATES_KEY` if present; data is public domain). Congress.gov API / govinfo for federal. `source_url` = the state legislature's own bill page (leginfo.legislature.ca.gov, nysenate.gov, legis.la.gov, le.utah.gov, mgaleg.maryland.gov, capitol.texas.gov, ilga.gov, flsenate.gov…) or congress.gov/govinfo; `text_url` = the official text; `status_url` = the official history page. Do not scrape state sites at volume — fetch only the pages for bills you are recording (≤1 req/s; NY Open Legislation may be CC BY-NC-ND — link it, do not copy). Leads: NCSL AI legislation database (archived tables on web.archive.org if live site blocks), FPF State of State AI 2025, MultiState, CDT, Steptoe tracker, ETO AGORA, Ballotpedia deepfake report, IAPP.

**Writing.** `bill_id`: `<jur>-<session>-<bill>` lower-case e.g. `ca-2025-sb-574`, `us-119-hr-7997`, `la-2025-hb-178`. `status_date` = date of `last_action`. `operative_sections`: 1–4 statements each citing the section ("§6068.1(a) B&P: attorneys must verify AI-generated citations before filing"). `excerpt`: verbatim operative text (public domain). `summary` 40–60 words: jurisdiction, bill, what the in-lane section does, status and date. Verify every bill on the primary page — AI search results invent bill numbers.
